Copy src and public over the corresponding folders in your Timberlite project. No SQL update is needed.
Gable Design/Options: tick Front Gable is above a doorway if applicable. The bar is also automatically included when the internal width exceeds 3800 mm.
Gable Summary > Metal Elements: tick Exclude to leave the bar out; untick it to include. The bar uses the usual quote pricing rules on a starting cost of GBP 76.50 (full stock length plus engineering).
Materials: the two bar prices are editable under Metal Elements. Set the white and foiled D-mould prices under Plastics to make them available as optional Summary extras.
