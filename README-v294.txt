Timberlite v294

Copy the included src and public folders over the corresponding folders in
your local Timberlite project. Restart the app if necessary.

Run retail-customer-v294.sql in the Timberlite Supabase SQL Editor after the
v275 customer-details setup. This adds the shared Retail account without
changing existing orders. Open Customers > Retail > Order History to see
orders recorded under the recognised Retail names.

In Schedule > To Order, open an affected checklist. Review the converted
whole lengths/sheets and save the checklist to update the monthly totals.
Already ordered lines and manual stock specifications are preserved.
Whole stock quantities are purchasing allowances, not a cutting layout.

In Schedule > Sales, expand the Partial warning under the affected figure
to see the job number, customer, reference and delivery date.
