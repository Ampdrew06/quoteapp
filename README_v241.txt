Timberlite v241 — Full-corner hip covering allowance
Apply over v240 by merging the included src folder into your local project. Back up first and restart with your API-enabled launcher.

Finding
The Victorian Hipped route previously followed the internal hip centre line until its first intersection with the outside tiling boundary. Different soffit offsets can put the outside eaves corner away from that line. The resulting length can omit the final corner allowance.

Change
The covering allowance now also measures travel from boss to full outside tiling corner, developed on each adjoining face. It retains the longest of those two distances and the existing centre-line requirement. This is a conservative supply allowance, NOT a validated physical hip cutting length. It does not redesign roof planes or resolve their different wallplate/setting-out conventions.
The Technical page now shows Centre-line run and Full corner allowance separately. Steel hip stock continues to use the existing shared 1230mm nominal cover / 30mm full-piece tolerance / pooled half-stock rules. Support lath and foam requirements follow the revised allowance. Slate hip units follow the revised allowance with their existing density rule; slate still has no steel support laths or foam.

Preserved
Actual roof geometry, bosses, measured facet settings, manual rafter layouts, member cuts/numbering, main tile surfaces/courses, field/internal laths and existing Summary adjustment/exclusion mechanisms. Reopened saved quotes use current calculations; review and save again if refreshing their stored price snapshot. Covering-related supply quantities and weights can change.
