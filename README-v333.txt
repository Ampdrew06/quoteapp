Timberlite v333 — Quotation Summary notes
Apply over v332, merging src and public as usual.

Quotation now has a Notes box below the price. Added Summary items and quantity
increases appear as extra supplied; individual exclusions appear as not included.
Quantity reductions are also described. An excluded or deleted optional extra
is omitted from the offer. Factory-only charges are not supply notes.

Whole omitted material categories are shown as Not Included in Order Details.
Included extras prevent an entire category being described as not included.
The Order Form repeats the supply notes and category exclusions.

Documents use the SAVED Summary selections and saved price together:
make Summary changes, return to Design/Options, Save Quote, then open Quotation.
Unsaved Summary changes do not alter an issued saved quotation.
No new glazing assumptions or manufacturing changes are introduced.
