Timberlite v304 — Double Hipped Edwardian rear valley materials

Merge the included src and public folders into the app project as with the
previous update, then restart the local app. No Supabase SQL is required.

In Design/Options, open Rear valley waterproofing review. Choose Bungalow
roof for OSB on both sides, or Sheer wall / Flat roof for Timberlite-side
OSB only. A 2,400 × 1,200 mm 18 mm OSB sheet yields four nominal 280 mm
strips for this estimate. The bungalow side allows 500 mm total extra.

Choose EPDM to price the full 1 m wide strips plus one 2.5 L deck adhesive
tub and one can of bonding adhesive. Check the 18 mm OSB sheet price,
EPDM price per square metre and both adhesive prices in Materials.
Choose GRP for OSB only; GRP materials are priced separately.

The EPDM estimator prefers a single stock length if it meets the allowance.
Otherwise it chooses the shortest qualifying combination, preferring fewer
pieces at equal total length. Default extra stock allowances are 350 mm
for a single strip and 850 mm total for joined strips; both can be changed
in the valley review and are saved with the quote. These are purchasing
allowances, not installation dimensions.

Example: 5,800 mm internal width and 6,240 mm external valley run gives
2 × 1 m by 3.6 m strips: 7,200 mm total, 960 mm extra. Estimated OSB is two
sheets against a bungalow roof or one sheet against a sheer wall or flat
roof. Check cuts and allowances for each job. Valley starter, rear hip
weathering and a valley cutting/installation list are outside this update.
