Timberlite v332 — Quotation and Order Form
Apply over v331, merging the included src and public folders into your app.
As admin, save a quote in Design/Options or open a saved quote or job, then open
Quotation or Order Form. These tabs use the saved design and price. Save any design
or price changes before issuing a document.

Quotation uses A4 landscape and reuses the Design/Options drawing component.
Order Form uses A4 portrait. It picks up the delivery address and required date
from the saved job when present. Edits on the form appear in the printed copy and
do not change the saved job. Blank lines can be completed on the printed form.
Use Print / Save as PDF, and turn off browser headers and footers if shown.
If a customer email is on file, Open email draft addresses your email. Attach
the PDF before sending. No mail is sent directly by the app in this version.

Supported: Lean-To, Hipped Lean-To, Gable, Edwardian, 3 and 5 Facet Victorian,
and Victorian Hipped Lean-To. Double Hipped Edwardian and Half/Half are unchanged.
