Timberlite v331 — Hipped Lean-To tile options
Apply over v330 using your usual update process. Merge the included src/ and public/ folders.
On Hipped Lean-To Design/Options as admin, select Metrotile then Burnt Umber.
Metrotile supplier carriage is £62 ex VAT by default. Enter an override (including £0 for carriage billed on a different quote).
Check your supplier rates in Materials; missing relevant rates are flagged before quoting.
Tapco is also available for admins. Enter its ridge/hip coverage and the required materials rates.
Half/Half remains unchanged pending confirmation of a universal cover.
